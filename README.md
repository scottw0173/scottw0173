### 👨‍💻 About Me

I spent six years building and running **Madness Reefing**, an aquarium installation and maintenance business in the OKC metro. The part I loved most turned out to be the systems thinking — recurring workflows, client operations, and making complex things run smoothly. Now I'm applying that operations mindset to data, backend, and AI-driven automation.

- 📊 I turn raw data into decisions — from **Power BI dashboards** on 100k+ records to serverless pipelines that rank and store results
- 🤖 I build **LLM-powered tools** on the Google Gemini API, and I'm extending them to orchestrate **multiple model providers**
- 🗄️ Comfortable moving data end-to-end: **API ingestion → ETL → SQL / DynamoDB storage → reporting**
- 🌱 Sharpening data structures & algorithms, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-agnostic architecture
- 🎯 Open to **Jr. Data Analyst** and **Technical Support Engineering** roles
- 💬 Always happy to talk shop — data, ops, backend, or AI
